We want everyone to be able to browse, buy and download from Project Store. The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Every page is checked with the axe-core accessibility engine, with animation on and with reduced motion, and the release is held until it reports no violations.
  • The whole purchase, from the product page to the paid order with its download link, works with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring on every control and a "Skip to content" link on every page.
  • Every text and background colour pair in the palette is checked for contrast by a script in the project.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are written next to the field and linked to it.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the scroll animations, the drifting shelf and the 3D background are not started. The home page's moving shelf has a Pause button, and it stops while the pointer is over it or while something inside it has keyboard focus. Copies of cards used to make the shelf loop are hidden from screen readers and from the Tab key.
  • Decorative drawings are hidden from screen readers.

Known limits

  • We have not yet tested with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack), or in Firefox and Safari.
  • The Google map on the contact block comes from Google and we cannot change how it behaves; the city is written next to it, and the "Open in Google Maps" link works without loading it.
  • Downloads are the projects' own files (source code and notes), which we have not checked for accessibility.
