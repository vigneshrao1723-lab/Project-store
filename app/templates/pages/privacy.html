We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we do not sell or rent personal data. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's own checkout and never reach this store.

Who else handles your data (processors)

  • Razorpay (payments, when real payments are switched on): receives the order number and amount, and the name, email and phone number on the order so its checkout can be prefilled. Razorpay processes your payment details itself. In the store's test mode no real payment is taken and nothing is sent to Razorpay.
  • The email provider (only if the store has switched on email messages): receives the address you chose and the message with your invoice and setup guide links.
  • Meta, through the WhatsApp Business Platform (only if the store has switched on WhatsApp messages): receives the number you chose, your first name, your order number and the links in the message.
  • Google Maps (only if you press "Load map" on the contact block): your browser then connects to Google, which receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies. Until you press the button nothing is requested from Google.
  • The server that hosts the store: stores the database and the logs described above.

Some of these companies may process data outside India. We use each only for the purpose stated.

Your rights under the DPDP Act

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and who we have shared it with (right to access information);
  • correct, complete or update it (right to correction);
  • erase it where we no longer need it for the purpose you gave it for or the law no longer requires us to keep it (right to erasure);
  • withdraw a consent you gave, for example to order messages, as easily as you gave it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • resolve a complaint (right to grievance redressal).

To use any of these rights, {{ reach() }}. We acknowledge every request within 48 hours and aim to complete it within one month. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Stopping order messages

Every invoice message has a "stop messages" link (/stop/...). Pressing its button puts that contact on the do-not-contact list at once, and we will never message it again. You can also simply ask us.

Deleting your data

Ask us as above. For an order we run the store's flask anonymise-order tool, which replaces the name on the order with "Removed" and blanks the phone, the chosen contact, and the recipient and text of every message for that order; the amounts, projects and dates stay, because they are the sales record. For an account we run flask anonymise-account, which does the same for every order of the account, empties the cart, and replaces the account's name, email and password so it can no longer be used or linked to you. Contact form messages are deleted on request.

Children

The store is meant for adults. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to buy for you; we do not knowingly collect a child's personal data.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, every form is protected against cross-site request forgery, sign-in and other actions are rate limited, and links in messages are signed and expire after seven days. If a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.
