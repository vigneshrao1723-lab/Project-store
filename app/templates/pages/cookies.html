Google Maps

The contact block shows a map of the city only if you press "Load map". Until then nothing is requested from Google. Once you load it, the map runs in a frame from Google, which may set its own cookies under Google's domains; those are covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one. The "Open in Google Maps" link takes you to Google's site.

Removing them

Signing out ends your session. You can delete this site's cookies and storage at any time in your browser's settings; you will then need to sign in again.
