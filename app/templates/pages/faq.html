What exactly do I get?
The project's source code, a setup guide and notes on how it is built. Each product page lists what its kit contains. See About.
How is it delivered?
As a download link on your order page, as soon as your payment is confirmed. Nothing is shipped. See the Delivery Policy.
Do I need an account?
Yes, to buy: your orders, downloads and invoices are kept under it. Browsing needs no account.
How do I pay?
Through Razorpay, with the methods its checkout offers you. Your card or UPI details go to Razorpay, never to this store. When a "Test mode" banner is showing, payments use a practice gateway and no real money moves.
Why does checkout say my copy is held?
Continuing to payment holds your copies for {{ reservation_minutes }} minutes, so nobody else can buy the last copy while you pay. If the payment does not arrive in time, the hold ends.
Can I cancel or get a refund?
An unpaid order can be cancelled any time. After payment, you can get a refund within {{ refund_days }} days if the download fails or the kit is materially not as described. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
Will you send me the invoice?
The invoice is always on your order page. At checkout you can also ask for it, with the setup guide, by email or WhatsApp; the message is sent once, after payment, if the store has switched that channel on.
How do I stop messages?
Every message has a "stop messages" link. Press its button and that contact is never messaged again.
Can you build something custom?
Ask: {{ reach() }}.
What do you do with my data?
Only what is needed to sell and deliver the kit; no analytics and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy.
