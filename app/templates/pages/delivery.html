Every kit is a digital product. There is no shipping, no shipping charge and no delivery address.

How you receive it

  • On your order page. As soon as your payment is confirmed, your order page (Orders, then the order) shows a download link for each project, the setup guide and the invoice. It stays there for as long as you have your account.
  • By email or WhatsApp, if you asked for it. If you ticked "Send my invoice and setup guide to this contact" at checkout and the store has switched that channel on, one message is sent after the payment is confirmed, never before. Its links to the invoice and the setup guide are signed and work for 7 days; the order page always works. The order page tells you truthfully whether the message was sent.

When

Straight away: the payment confirmation from Razorpay (or the practice gateway in test mode) marks the order paid and the links appear. If a project's download link is not ready yet, the order page says "Link coming soon"; {{ reach() }} and we will send it.

If something goes wrong

If a link does not work, {{ reach() }} with your order number. A download that fails and cannot be fixed is covered by the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
