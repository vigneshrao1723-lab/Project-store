Project Store sells digital project kits. Because a kit is delivered the moment your payment is confirmed, the rules are different from a parcel. This page says exactly when you can cancel or get your money back.

Cancelling before you pay

An order you have not paid for yet can be cancelled at any time with "Cancel this order and return to cart" on the payment page. The copies go back on the shelf and nothing is charged. If you simply leave, the hold ends by itself after {{ reservation_minutes }} minutes.

Refunds after payment

You can get a full refund within {{ refund_days }} days of payment if:

  • the download fails: the link on your order page does not work and we cannot give you a working one; or
  • the kit is materially not as described: what you received is substantially different from what its product page says is in the kit.

Otherwise a kit is not refundable once it has been delivered, because a digital copy cannot be returned.

One case is refunded automatically: if your payment arrives after your hold has ended and the project has sold out in the meantime, the order is marked for a refund and the full amount is returned.

How to ask

To ask, {{ reach() }} within {{ refund_days }} days of payment, with your order number (it starts with "PS-") and what went wrong. We acknowledge your request within 48 hours and tell you our decision within 7 days. If we refund, the money goes back to the payment method you used, through Razorpay; banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it.

Orders paid in the store's test mode used a practice gateway; no real money moved, so there is nothing to refund.

Complaints
